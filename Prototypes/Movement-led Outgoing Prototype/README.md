# Movement-led Outgoing Prototype

Separate prototype for the PRD "Manual Outgoing Movement Booking and Classification" (`New Requirements/PRD - Manual Outgoing Movement Booking and Classification.md`). Scope is movement-led outgoing, plus one incoming classification, **Others**, used as the correcting entry for a mistake. The other incoming classifications are a later phase. Open `index.html`. Pure HTML, JS and CSS. Data is kept in this browser (key `mlo-si-v10`), separate from the Ext Trade Settlement prototype; **Reset demo data** starts over. Use **View as** to switch between Alice and Bob (two operators), and **Advance 1 day** to move the demo date.

Layout follows the Operator UI repo: Roboto, antd tokens, 640px single-column forms with titled sections, a Token / Fiat Type radio, a Payment rail filter and column on the Fiat list, and two-column detail pages with a grey label above a bold value.

## What is in it
| Sidebar item | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Approvals | Maker Checker list of movement requests (book and classify) |
| Movement Management > Outgoing Movement | Token and Fiat lists, detail, **Book Outgoing Movement**, **Classify** (two separate steps, each approved by a second operator) |
| Movement Management > Incoming Movement | Token and Fiat lists, detail, **Book Incoming Movement** (facts only) and **Classify** with **Others** only. This is the correcting entry (PRD FR-7). Deposit and Confirm are not simulated |
| Prototype only: Balances, Demo controls | Account and venue balances, Unclassified outgoing with the age of the oldest entry (flagged after 7 days), unclassified incoming (including unknown funds awaiting refund), company costs (net of company credits), recent balance effects, and a "what to try" script |

Greyed items (the rest of the Operator UI menu) are not part of this prototype.

## Flow
1. **Detail pages** copy the repo's outgoing movement pages, same sections, rows and order. Token: Details (two columns), Additional Details, Know Your Transactions. Fiat: Details (two columns), Payment Details, Whitelist Bank Account Details. A booked-by-hand movement keeps every row and shows "Not applicable (booked by hand)" where the value does not exist. One extra section, Booking and Classification, holds Origin, Charge type, Charged to, Related transaction, Explanation and Internal note.
2. **Book** the facts only: Venue and asset (Type radio, Venue, Asset; the venue's current balance is shown, and the bank account number, blockchain and source address fill in automatically), Amount and timing for fiat, or Transaction Details for token (Amount, Transacted at, Reference, and for token the optional Network fee), Other details (payment rail ACT, FAST, MEPS or TT (choosing MEPS or TT fills in the charge bearer and a DBS charge bearer account from the System > Charge Instructions default, which can be changed), beneficiary or destination, note). No classification on the form. The review modal states the effect on the venue balance. A booking that would take the venue below zero is allowed and shows a warning to the maker and the checker.
3. A second operator approves (the approval toast has an **Open movement** button). The movement is `confirmed` and **Unclassified**; the venue drops and the amount is listed under **Unclassified outgoing**.
4. **Classify** it (Related transaction is required for the Network fee charge type, and once chosen a Linked transaction details panel shows what it is, with a warning if its venue differs; a Related transaction that matches a movement, or a withdrawal ID such as WD-2041, is saved as a real link and shown under Linked movements on the target; anything else is kept as text): Venue charges (Bank charge, Network fee, Other venue fee), Refund (of an unknown funds entry; once chosen, a panel shows the amount to refund, whether this movement matches it, the venue, date, original sender and reference) or Others (explanation of at least 10 characters). Then who bears it: an account (balance falls, monthly statement line) or the custodian (recorded as a company cost). A second operator approves. If it is tied to an account, that account's balance changes and the movement appears on its monthly statement (Balances shows the statement lines); a custodian-borne classification changes no account. No separate transaction or menu is created: the classified movement is the record.
5. Booked-by-hand movements have no execution method (the repo's `manual` means a withdrawal needing manual processing); use the **Origin** filter on the outgoing lists to find them.
6. Withdrawal, Rebalance and Settlement are instruction-led and are never offered for a manual booking.

## Token specifics (checked against the repo)
- The Token list has a KYT status column, as the repo does; a hand-booked movement shows "-".
- The Blockchain and addresses card also has Amount type and External ID, as the repo's token detail does; a hand-booked movement shows Amount type as not applicable and hides External ID.
- The optional Network fee field always shows on a token booking, on every chain. A hint says to leave it empty when a fee sponsor paid (for example on Ethereum).
- Execution method shows "Not applicable (booked by hand)" (the repo would show Automated when the field is empty).

## Venues
Two fiat venues (Bank Venue V and X, bank accounts) and three token venues, each with one blockchain and one address (Token Venue W, a Bitcoin hot wallet; Token Venue Z, an Ethereum hot wallet; Cold Wallet Y, a Bitcoin cold wallet). The Token booking form follows the repo's create page: Blockchain first, then a Venue (hot and cold wallets on that blockchain only), then Source Address (filled in), optional Destination Address, then Asset with a Balance row (Total and Available), Amount, Transaction Hash, Transacted At (UTC) and, on Bitcoin, Network Fee. The Fiat form asks for the venue first and fills in the bank account number. The booking offers fiat venues for Fiat and hot or cold wallets for Token; external wallets are not a source. Detail pages show **From** (the venue, with its kind) and **To** (the whitelisted destination for instruction-led, or what was typed with an "Entered by hand" tag). Balances shows each venue's type.

## Notes
- Audit events follow the PRD: `outgoing_movement:create_request` and `classify_request` when the maker submits, then `create` and `classify` when the checker approves. A rejection writes no business row.
- **Correcting a mistake (PRD UC-8, FR-7):** book an incoming movement of the same amount at the same venue, approve it, then classify it **Others** with an explanation that names the original (credited to an account, or kept by us as company income). The original is also classified Others. There is no special screen and no edit.
- **Unknown funds** are an incoming movement that is `detected` and `unclassified` (as in the repo). A Refund moves one to status **Refunded**; an incoming Others clears it too. The Refund list offers only entries at the same venue and in the same asset, and once an entry is chosen the form compares the destination entered at booking with the original sender (a warning on a mismatch, a note when none was entered), and a pending Refund and a pending Others on the same entry block each other.
- A tied line is dated with the movement's transacted date, so it falls in the month it happened.
- The folder started as a copy of the Ext Trade Settlement prototype. Code that nothing in this prototype can reach (settlement instructions, counterparties, whitelists, the incoming forms and the settlement approval types) has been removed, so the folder is now `index.html`, `styles.css`, `shared.js`, `shell.js` (sidebar, routing, approvals list) and `si.js` (movements, balances, approvals detail, demo data). Unused CSS was removed too.
- Simplified in the prototype, specified in the PRD: audit rows are one-line text (not the structured metadata, security fields or failure rows); only the operator role gate exists (no Compliance user, no account-active check); instruction-led actions (Confirm, Mark as failed, Manually process, Override KYT, BTC fee rate) are not simulated; the venue pages that embed the outgoing list, the withdrawal and rebalance links, `borneBy`, decimals and active-venue checks, and the catalogue as data are not built. There is no incoming booking or classification in the prototype. Two incoming records are seeded so the demo has something real: IM-3001 (a confirmed 5,000 SGD deposit) and IM-3005 (200 USD of unknown funds, `detected` and unclassified, which the Refund demo clears). Incoming booking in the prototype is simplified to venue, asset, amount, time, reference, sender and note. 
- The PRD section **Step-by-Step Use Cases** (UC-1 to UC-8) walks through each option with the same demo data. `Notes/Test Guide - Movement-led Outgoing Prototype.md` is the click-by-click test version.
